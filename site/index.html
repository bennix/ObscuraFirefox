THE WORKSPACE / 01

More room
for what matters.

01

From a prompt to a browser action.

Read pages, add attachments and guide multi-step tasks through a local browser-control bridge. A task observer checks progress. Connect your own ZenMux key.

02

Find the media. Choose your format.

Right-click X or YouTube media to download. Inspect formats and download HLS segments with merging and progress in Firefox’s download list. Availability depends on the website and account access.

03

Privacy, with visible controls.

WebRTC disabled by default, Firefox fingerprint protection, partitioned cookies and proxy-aware requests. AI task content is sent to your provider as needed. No promise of anonymity.

04

Read in your own rhythm.

Batched immersive translation, contextual word lookup and a local dictionary. The workspace follows Firefox’s theme and UI language, with English as the fallback.
