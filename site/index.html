THE WORKSPACE / 01

More room
for what matters.

01

From a prompt to a browser action.

Read pages, add attachments and guide multi-step tasks through a local browser-control bridge. Trusted browser input, post-action page snapshots and a task observer help check progress and completion. Connect your own ZenMux key.

02

Find the media. Choose your format.

Right-click X or YouTube media, choose quality and merge video with audio automatically. X discovery follows visible media and supports clear/rescan, with progress in Firefox’s download list. Availability depends on the website and account access.

03

Privacy, with visible controls.

On macOS and Linux, automatic mode follows an active system tunnel. When a local proxy needs no password, Obscura can use it for webpages, AI and downloads. Firefox fingerprint protection and partitioned cookies are enabled, with WebRTC disabled by default. AI task content is sent to your provider as needed. No promise of anonymity.

04

Read in your own rhythm.

Batched immersive translation, contextual word lookup and a local dictionary. The workspace follows Firefox’s theme and UI language, with English as the fallback.
